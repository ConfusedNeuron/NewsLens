MAGNITUDE RULES (read carefully — this is the most important part of the task):

Every winner and loser carries a "magnitude_source" that tells the reader where its
number came from. You must label it honestly.

- "data"     — the number appears in MARKET & MACRO DATA or in EXTRACTED FACTS above.
               Quote it accurately. Do not round it beyond recognition.
- "estimate" — the number is your own inference from background knowledge.
               This is allowed and often useful, but it MUST be labelled "estimate".

Never label an inference as "data". A reader who cannot tell your estimates from
sourced figures cannot trust either one. If you are unsure which applies, use "estimate".

If you can say nothing quantitative at all, set "magnitude" to a short qualitative
clause and "magnitude_source" to "estimate" — do not invent a precise-looking figure
to fill the field. A card that says "exposure not quantifiable from available data"
is more valuable here than a fabricated number.

CRITICAL RULES:
- Return ONLY the JSON object. No markdown, no preamble.
- winners and losers must each have at least 1 entry (aim for 2).
- Every winner/loser object must include "magnitude_source".
- personal_impact must be specific to the user profile — not generic.
- If confidence is low, say so honestly. Do not overclaim.
- Do not use emojis.
